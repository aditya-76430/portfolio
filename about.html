My Story

It started with a simple curiosity about how technology could make everyday work easier. Working with data, inventory, billing, and supply chain operations made me realize how much can be improved with the right software.

Alongside my BCA, I started learning programming and exploring Python, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, SQL, and data analytics. What began with small programs and experiments slowly turned into building real projects and my own web applications.

From working with Excel, Power BI, Tally, and ERP systems to learning how to build applications from scratch, every project has taught me something new. I’m still learning, experimenting, breaking things, and building them again.
